RUN 01 · THE COACH

01 / 03 · SCROLL OR CLICK

Three days
is enough

I've walked this road plenty of times myself — rebuilt my technique, scrapped it, started again from nothing. What I learned is that you don't get far on your own. You always need a coach, an outside eye on what you're actually doing. That's exactly why I'm here.

Twenty years of riding and every stage of it behind me, my job as a coach comes down to two things: get you to the trick you're after faster, and keep the injuries and the bad habits out of your technique on the way.

So: small group, three days, sessions split by level. We film everything, we go through it in the evening, and you go back up the next morning knowing what to fix. No drills you'll never use, no lecture. Just airtime and honest feedback.

The camp is for beginners and progressing riders who want to push their freestyle. One requirement — you're confident on a snowboard.

QUESTIONS

What level do I need to be?

The camp works for beginners in the park and for riders already progressing. There is one real requirement: you're confident on a snowboard and in control on a blue line. Everything past that we build from where you actually are — sessions are split by level, so nobody is queuing behind riders twice their speed.

Do I need to land tricks already?

No. Plenty of people arrive having never left the ground. That's what the small airbag is for — you can commit to a rotation without paying for it on landing, and we move you up to the medium bag only when the take-off is repeatable.

What does a day look like?

Warm-up at 09:00, a three-hour session on the small bag from 11:00, a break, then a second session on small and medium from 14:30 to 17:00. Stretching at 19:00 and video review at 20:00. Sunday is a short day: warm-up at 08:00 and one session from 09:00 to 12:00.

Can I rent gear there?

Yes, there's a rental at the base, and I can reserve a park-friendly board if you tell me your size when you register. Bring your own if you have one — three days on a board you know is worth more.

I'm coming on my own. Is that fine?

Most people do. Five or six riders in a group and three days together — you won't be riding alone by the first afternoon. I can also put you in touch with others looking to share a room.

What if the conditions are bad?

If the airbag can't run, we move the day or refund it in full — no argument. Rain and flat light we ride through; that's normal here and it's still useful.

Refunds and cancellation?

Free cancellation up to two weeks before. After that I'll refund whatever I can fill from the waiting list. Injury with a doctor's note, full refund at any point.

Why only five or six riders?

Because I film everyone, watch every attempt, and go through the footage with each of you in the evening. Past six that stops being possible and it turns into a lesson with an audience.

Is food included?

No. Lunch is in the break, and most evenings we eat together after the review and split the bill — usually 15–20 € a head.

What do I leave with?

Your filmed tricks from the last day, and a short written plan for your next few training sessions — what to work on, in what order, and what to stop doing.

Can a non-riding partner come along?

Sure. They're welcome at the evening review and dinner, and Klínovec has plenty to do off the hill. Just tell me so I book the right table.
