03 — GRADING

The tier list is the product

Every candidate we touch gets a grade, and the grade is written down before anyone talks price. You only ever see S and A. The name of the company comes from the top of this list.

S — ships without supervision

Has already solved your class of problem in production, at your scale or above, and can explain the decisions they would make differently now.

  • Defends an architecture choice under pressure without getting defensive
  • Two references from engineers who worked beside them, not managers who liked them
  • Explains their work in language a non-engineer can follow
  • Owns a failure in the interview without being asked twice

Typically 3 of these on a shortlist. If we cannot find three, we tell you that instead of padding.

A — strong hire inside a team

Everything an S does, with one dependency: a tech lead, a review culture, or a domain they have not seen before. Most great hires live here.

  • Clears the technical screen without hints
  • Growth curve is visible across their last two roles
  • Needs an existing team to plug into rather than a green field
  • Forwarded with a written note on what support they will need

We say out loud which of your seats an A fits and which they do not.

B — right seat, wrong level

Good engineers who do not match this brief. Usually a seniority mismatch, sometimes a stack we cannot verify yet.

  • Held in our own pipeline with their grade and the reason attached
  • Re-graded when they ship something that changes the answer
  • Offered to you only if you open a role they actually fit
  • Never re-sent under a different job title hoping you forgot

A B today is often an A in eighteen months. We keep the note either way.

C — not what the brief asked for

The CV never reaches you. This is the whole point of paying an agency, and it is the part most agencies skip.

  • Could not produce work matching what the CV claims
  • Screen answers did not survive one follow-up question
  • Applying to everything, targeting nothing
  • Rejected with a reason, in writing, to the candidate

We do not forward C-grade candidates to fill a quota, and we do not bill for volume.

04 — PROCESS

From brief to signed, in the open

The same four steps whether you are hiring a staff engineer or handing us a delivery. You see the pipeline the whole way through.

  1. 01

    Scope call

    Day 0 · 45 minutes

    What the seat actually does, who it reports to, what the money is, and what would make you say no. For consulting, the same call establishes the deliverable and the constraint that matters most.

  2. 02

    Search & screen

    Day 1–7

    Sourcing, then a technical screen run by an engineer against the rubric agreed on the scope call. Every candidate gets a grade and a written reason, including the ones you never see.

  3. 03

    Shortlist

    Day 7–12

    Three names, each with the screen notes, the grade, the salary expectation, and the risk we think you are taking. You interview, we schedule, nobody chases anybody.

  4. 04

    Offer & landing

    Day 12 onward

    Offer strategy, counter-offer handling, and a check-in at week one and month three. Placements carry a replacement guarantee — the term is in the agreement, not on a website.

09 — FAQ

The questions we get asked first

What happens if the hire does not work out?

Permanent placements carry a replacement guarantee: if the person leaves or is let go inside the guarantee window, we run the search again at no additional fee. The window and the conditions are written into the agreement before you pay anything.

Do you send CVs we have already seen?

No. Before the search starts we take your list of candidates already in your pipeline and exclude them. If we surface someone you have seen before, we tell you where they came from rather than claiming the introduction.

Who runs the technical screen?

An engineer who has worked in that discipline, against a rubric we agree with you on the scope call. Recruiters run the process; they do not decide whether someone can do the job.

Can you work with our internal recruiters instead of replacing them?

Yes, and it is often the cheaper answer. We can run screening as a service on your own pipeline, or embed a recruiter with your team for a fixed period, without touching your ATS ownership.

Are your consultants employees or contractors?

Both, depending on the market and the engagement. Either way the contracting, compliance, and invoicing sit with us — you get one agreement and one invoice.

What do you do with candidate data?

We hold it to run the search you briefed and to grade candidates in our own pipeline, we do not sell it, and candidates can ask us to delete it at any time. The full statement lives in the privacy policy.
