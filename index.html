I spent years throwing myself at the same jump and landing the same way. What changed it wasn't more laps. It was somebody standing at the takeoff telling me exactly what my back foot was doing.

That's what I do here. Small group, one park, three days. We film every hit, we watch it over dinner, and you go back up in the morning knowing what to fix. No drills you'll never use, no lecture. Just laps and honest feedback.

If you can ride a blue line comfortably and you've been circling the same box or the same 180 for a season, this camp is for you.

QUESTIONS

What level do I need to be?

You should be able to ride any blue line in control and have hit a small box or side hit before. Beyond that, come as you are — I split the group by what people are working on, not by label.

Can I rent gear there?

Yes, there's a rental at the base, and I can reserve a park-friendly board if you tell me your size when you register. Bring your own if you have one — three days on a board you know is worth more.

I'm coming on my own. Is that fine?

Most people do. Twelve riders, one park, shared dinners — you won't be riding alone by the first afternoon. I can also put you in touch with others looking to share a room.

What if the conditions are bad?

If the park can't run, we move the day or refund it in full — no argument. Rain and flat light we ride through; that's normal here and it's still useful.

Refunds and cancellation?

Free cancellation up to two weeks before. After that I'll refund whatever I can fill from the waiting list. Injury with a doctor's note, full refund at any point.

Why only twelve riders?

Because I film everyone and review the footage with everyone. Past twelve, that stops being possible and it turns into a lesson.

Is food included?

No. We eat together in the evening and split the bill — usually 15–20 € a head. Lunch is on the hill, whenever the session breaks.

Can a non-riding partner come along?

Sure. They're welcome at the evening review and dinner, and Klínovec has plenty to do off the hill. Just tell me so I book the right table.
